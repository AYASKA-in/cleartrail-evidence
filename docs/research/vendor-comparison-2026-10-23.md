# Public product study: Vanta, Drata and ClearTrail

Research date: 23 October 2026. Reference: the shared SMB evidence-collection workflow, with a free, independently implemented product as the objective.

## Scope and evidence quality

This study applies the requested reverse-engineer-anything research workflow. It reuses the existing [reference ledger](../REFERENCE_RESEARCH.md) and [capture manifest](manifest.json), reads current official vendor help documentation, and adds one focused REA observation of a public Drata documentation page. No authenticated Vanta or Drata feature was exercised. All application capabilities below are **vendor-described**, not independently verified product behavior. Feature availability may depend on plan, add-on and interface version.

ClearTrail's comparison baseline is the current source review: 14 original controls, comprising 11 automated checks across GitHub, Google Workspace and AWS, plus three manual attestations. Hosted authentication, tenant roles, encrypted credentials, isolated collection, durable on-demand jobs, history and verified snapshot exports exist. These are foundations, rather than evidence of full compliance-program coverage. Source locations include `cleartrail/catalog.py`, `core.py`, `hosted.py`, `hosted_repository.py` and `reports.py`.

## What matters for the free product

The largest functional gap is the lifecycle around evidence: collect repeatedly, establish the relevant population and period, assign a responsible person, review changes and exceptions, retain the exact evidence version, and share a scoped package. Merely expanding the number of configuration checks will not close those workflow gaps.

These workflows can use open-source components and original application code without a mandatory AI model or paid scanner service. Prefer structured imports and deterministic rules before optional AI assistance. Free software does not make a multi-customer hosted service costless: compute, storage, backups, third-party accounts, operations and independent audits still require resources. This research verifies neither current vendor pricing nor an investment return.

An evidence bundle supports audit preparation. It does not itself issue a SOC 2 report, ISO certificate, complete an ISMS, establish operating effectiveness throughout a period, or guarantee auditor acceptance. Detailed compliance coverage and required samples should be agreed with the intended auditor.
